HOUSE RENOVATION IN LAHORE

We'll tell you what's actually wrong with an old house — not just what's easy to fix.

Renovation work is where honesty matters most. Old Lahore homes often hide real structural issues behind fresh paint. We inspect properly before quoting, so you know if you're fixing a wall or fixing the reason the wall keeps cracking.

Book an Inspection WhatsApp Us

What renovation work we take on

  • Structural repair of cracked or weakened walls, beams and columns
  • Damp-proofing and treatment of seepage and water damage
  • Re-plastering and re-wiring/re-plumbing where required
  • Removing and replacing old flooring with new tiles or marble
  • Adding a room, floor, or boundary wall extension to an existing house
  • Full "gut renovation" of an old house — down to structure, then rebuilt inside

How we inspect before quoting

Engineer Tajamal Malik checks the structure — cracks, dampness, foundation movement, roof and beam condition — before we give any estimate. If a wall looks cosmetic but is actually a symptom of a structural or drainage problem, we say so, even if it means a bigger scope and cost than the client expected. We would rather lose a job by being honest than win it and leave a client with the same problem in two years.

Working in an occupied home

Many renovation jobs happen while a family is still living in the house. We plan work in stages — room by room or floor by floor where possible — and discuss dust, water and electricity cut-off timing with the family in advance rather than surprising them.

Old vs. new construction standards

Older Lahore houses were often built to different standards (thinner walls, different foundation depth, older material). Where we tie new work into an old structure, our engineer accounts for this rather than assuming the old structure will simply support new load the same way a fresh structure would.

What we build and renovate

  • Shop shells — structure, frontage and internal walls for a new shop
  • Showroom fit-outs, including flooring (tiles/marble) and finishing
  • Small office and commercial building construction
  • Renovation of existing shops — new front, flooring, electrical upgrade
  • Boundary and structural work for plazas and small commercial plots

Working around a running business

For renovation of an already-operating shop, we plan work to minimise closed days — sequencing noisy or messy work first, and finishing/detailing work later, and discussing working hours with the shopkeeper so deliveries, customers and construction don't clash more than necessary.

Budget-focused construction

Commercial clients are usually working to a firmer budget and payback timeline than a homeowner. We are direct about where money is best spent (structure, wiring capacity, flooring durability for foot traffic) versus where a shopkeeper can reasonably save (decorative finishing that can be upgraded later).

Compliance

Any approvals, permissions or regulatory requirements for commercial construction in your specific area of Lahore are the client's responsibility to confirm with the relevant authority; we build to the agreed plan and specification set out in the written contract.
